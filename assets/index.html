Brief Bio

Tianyu Han received the BEng degree from Northeastern University (Qinhuangdao) in 2022 and the MSc degree from the University of Warwick in 2023. He is currently pursuing the PhD degree with the School of Engineering, University of Warwick. He is a Student Member of the IEEE.

His current research interests include wireless communications, signal processing, channel inference and reconstruction, distributed wireless networks, and satellite communications.

Research Interests

Wireless Communication Theory Analytical modeling, outage analysis, and mathematically interpretable system design.
Channel Inference & Reconstruction Recovering useful channel information from partial observations, historical CSI, and physical structure.
Distributed Wireless Networks Cell-free architectures, interference management, and network-level performance analysis.
Satellite Communications Compact receive architectures, multi-user satellite links, and non-terrestrial communication systems.
