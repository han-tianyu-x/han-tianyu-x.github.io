Cell-Free Fluid Antenna Multiple Access Networks

T. Han, Y. Zhu, K.-K. Wong, G. Zheng, and H. Shin
IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, 2025
DOI arXiv Back to publications

Research Question

How can reconfigurable user antennas be integrated into a distributed cell-free network while maintaining scalable interference management?

Summary

This paper extends fluid-antenna multiple access to a distributed cell-free architecture and analyzes desired-signal, interference, and outage behavior under different port-switching strategies.

Main Ideas

  • Extends FAMA analysis from a local link to a distributed cell-free network.
  • Analyzes fast and slow port-switching strategies.
  • Characterizes outage behavior and identifies regimes where slow switching is advantageous.

Citation
