Profile

Personal academic identity.

I received my BEng degree from Northeastern University (Qinhuangdao) in 2022 and my MSc degree from the University of Warwick in 2023. I am currently pursuing a PhD degree in Engineering at the University of Warwick. I am also a Student Member of IEEE.

My broader goal is to understand how future wireless systems can exploit spatial, temporal, and statistical structure more effectively — especially when full channel information is difficult or expensive to obtain.

Research

Questions I work on.

01 · Theory

Wireless communication theory

Analytical performance modeling, outage behavior, and mathematically interpretable system design.
02 · Inference

Channel inference & reconstruction

Recovering useful channel structure from incomplete observations, historical CSI, and known physical constraints.
03 · Networks

Distributed wireless networks

Cell-free systems, interference management, and scalable architectures across many distributed nodes.
04 · Satellite

Satellite & non-terrestrial systems

Compact arrays, multi-user satellite communications, and future non-terrestrial network architectures.
