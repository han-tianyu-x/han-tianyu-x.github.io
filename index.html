Brief Bio

Tianyu Han received the BEng degree from Northeastern University (Qinhuangdao) in 2022, and the MSc degree from the University of Warwick in 2023. He is currently pursuing the PhD degree with the School of Engineering, University of Warwick. He is a Student Member of the IEEE.

His current research interests include wireless communications, signal processing, channel inference and reconstruction, distributed wireless networks, and satellite communications.

Research Interests

Wireless Communication Theory
Analytical modeling, outage analysis, and mathematically interpretable system design.
Channel Inference & Reconstruction
Using partial observations, historical CSI, and structure-aware methods to infer missing channel information.
Distributed Wireless Networks
Cell-free architectures, interference management, and large-scale system analysis.
Satellite Communications
Compact receive architectures, multi-user satellite links, and non-terrestrial communication systems.
