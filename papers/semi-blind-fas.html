Semi-Blind Fluid Antenna System: Port Selection via Statistical Analysis

T. Han, Y. Zhu, K.-K. Wong, G. Zheng, C.-B. Chae, and X. You
IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, 2026
DOI arXiv Back to publications

Research Question

How can a spatially reconfigurable receiver select a good port when complete instantaneous CSI is unavailable?

Summary

This paper develops a semi-blind statistical framework that uses incomplete historical CSI to infer the conditional distribution of the channel and select a statistically favorable port without requiring full instantaneous CSI.

Main Ideas

  • Builds a spatial-temporal statistical model from incomplete historical observations.
  • Uses conditional distribution analysis rather than full instantaneous CSI.
  • Provides a lightweight and scalable port-selection framework without deep-learning pre-training.

Citation
