Semi-Blind Fluid Antenna System: Port Selection via Statistical Analysis

T. Han, Y. Zhu, K.-K. Wong, G. Zheng, C.-B. Chae, and X. You
IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications 2026
Open DOI ↗ Open arXiv ↗
Research overview

What question does this work ask?

How can a spatially reconfigurable receiver select a good port when complete instantaneous CSI is not available?

What does the paper do?

The paper develops a semi-blind statistical framework that uses incomplete historical CSI to infer the conditional channel distribution and identify the statistically optimal port.

Figure 1 from the open arXiv version: geometry and spatial-temporal structure of the semi-blind statistical port-selection framework.

Main ideas

  • Builds a spatial-temporal statistical framework for incomplete observations.
  • Uses conditional distribution analysis instead of full instantaneous CSI.
  • Provides a lightweight, scalable strategy without pre-training or deep-learning structures.
