Semi-Blind Fluid Antenna System: Port Selection via Statistical Analysis

T. Han, Y. Zhu, K.-K. Wong, G. Zheng, C.-B. Chae, and X. You
IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, 2026

DOI arXiv Back to publications

Research Question

How can a spatially reconfigurable receiver select a good port when complete instantaneous CSI is not available?

Summary

The paper develops a semi-blind statistical framework that uses incomplete historical CSI to infer the conditional channel distribution and identify the statistically optimal port.

Citation

T. Han, Y. Zhu, K.-K. Wong, G. Zheng, C.-B. Chae, and X. You, “Semi-Blind Fluid Antenna System: Port Selection via Statistical Analysis,” IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, 2026. DOI: 10.1109/TWC.2026.3722171.
