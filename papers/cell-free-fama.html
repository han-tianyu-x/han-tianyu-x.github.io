Cell-Free Fluid Antenna Multiple Access Networks

T. Han, Y. Zhu, K.-K. Wong, G. Zheng, and H. Shin
IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications 2025
Open DOI ↗ Open arXiv ↗
Research overview

What question does this work ask?

How can reconfigurable user antennas be integrated into a distributed cell-free network while maintaining analytical tractability and useful interference management?

What does the paper do?

This paper develops a cell-free multiple-access model where each user is served by its nearest base station and uses a reconfigurable antenna to mitigate inter-user interference.

Figure 1 from the open arXiv version: conceptual cell-free network model with distributed base stations and reconfigurable user antennas.

Main ideas

  • Extends the multiple-access framework from a single-cell setting to a geographically distributed cell-free architecture.
  • Analyzes desired-signal and interference behavior under fast and slow port switching.
  • Characterizes outage performance and identifies regimes where slow switching is particularly effective.
