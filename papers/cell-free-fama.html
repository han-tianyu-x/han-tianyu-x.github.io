Cell-Free Fluid Antenna Multiple Access Networks

T. Han, Y. Zhu, K.-K. Wong, G. Zheng, and H. Shin
IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, 2025

DOI arXiv Back to publications

Research Question

How can reconfigurable user antennas be integrated into a distributed cell-free network while maintaining analytical tractability and useful interference management?

Summary

This work extends the multiple-access analysis from a single-cell setting to a geographically distributed cell-free architecture. It studies desired-signal and interference behavior under fast and slow port switching and characterizes outage performance.

Citation

T. Han, Y. Zhu, K.-K. Wong, G. Zheng, and H. Shin, “Cell-Free Fluid Antenna Multiple Access Networks,” IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, 2025. DOI: 10.1109/TWC.2025.3559441.
