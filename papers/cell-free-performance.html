Performance Analysis of Fluid Antenna System in Cell-Free Networks

T. Han, Y. Zhu, G. Zheng, and H. Shin
2025 IEEE Globecom Workshops, 2025
DOI Back to publications

Research Question

What performance gains can reconfigurable user antennas provide when deployed in a cell-free network?

Summary

This conference paper presents an analytical performance study of fluid-antenna operation in a cell-free network setting and provides an early foundation for the later cell-free multiple-access work.

Main Ideas

  • Studies fluid-antenna behavior in a distributed cell-free architecture.
  • Focuses on analytical performance characterization.
  • Provides a conference-stage foundation for the broader cell-free research direction.
