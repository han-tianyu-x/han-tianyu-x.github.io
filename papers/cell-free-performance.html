Performance Analysis of Fluid Antenna System in Cell-Free Networks

T. Han, Y. Zhu, G. Zheng, and H. Shin
2025 IEEE Globecom Workshops 2025
Open DOI ↗
Research overview

What question does this work ask?

What performance gains can reconfigurable user antennas provide when they operate in a distributed cell-free network?

What does the paper do?

This conference paper provides an earlier performance-analysis treatment of reconfigurable antenna operation in a cell-free network setting.

No openly accessible primary-source Figure 1 is embedded for this conference paper in the current package.

Main ideas

  • Studies reconfigurable antenna behavior in a distributed cell-free architecture.
  • Focuses on analytical performance characterization.
  • Provides a conference-stage foundation for the broader cell-free research direction.
