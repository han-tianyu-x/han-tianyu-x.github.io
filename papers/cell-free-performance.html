Performance Analysis of Fluid Antenna System in Cell-Free Networks

T. Han, Y. Zhu, G. Zheng, and H. Shin
2025 IEEE Globecom Workshops

DOI Back to publications

Research Question

What performance gains can reconfigurable user antennas provide when they operate in a distributed cell-free network?

Summary

This conference paper provides an earlier analytical treatment of reconfigurable antenna behavior in a cell-free network setting and serves as a foundation for later journal work.

Citation
