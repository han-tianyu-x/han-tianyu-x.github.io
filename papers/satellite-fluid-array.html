Fluid Antenna Enabled Compact Ultra Massive Antenna Array for Satellite Communications

T. Han, Y. Zhu, G. Zheng, and P.-D. M. Arapoglou
IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications, 2026

DOI arXiv Back to publications

Research Question

Can a compact multi-port receive architecture improve multi-user satellite uplink performance while keeping RF complexity controlled?

Summary

This paper develops a compact ultra-massive receive architecture for satellite communications and derives analytical characterizations of signal power, interference power, outage probability, and ergodic rate.

Citation

T. Han, Y. Zhu, G. Zheng, and P.-D. M. Arapoglou, “Fluid Antenna Enabled Compact Ultra Massive Antenna Array for Satellite Communications,” IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications, 2026. DOI: 10.1109/JSAC.2025.3616279.
