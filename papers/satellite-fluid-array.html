Fluid Antenna Enabled Compact Ultra Massive Antenna Array for Satellite Communications

T. Han, Y. Zhu, G. Zheng, and P.-D. M. Arapoglou
IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications, 2026
DOI arXiv Back to publications

Research Question

Can a compact multi-port receive architecture improve multi-user satellite uplink performance while keeping RF-chain complexity under control?

Summary

This work develops a compact ultra-massive receive architecture for satellite communications and analytically characterizes desired signal power, interference, outage probability, and ergodic rate.

Main Ideas

  • Introduces a compact multi-port receive architecture for satellite uplinks.
  • Derives analytical performance expressions for signal, interference, outage, and rate.
  • Explains how compact spatial structures can provide useful beamforming gains.
