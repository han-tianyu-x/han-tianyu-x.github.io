Fluid Antenna Enabled Compact Ultra Massive Antenna Array for Satellite Communications

T. Han, Y. Zhu, G. Zheng, and P.-D. M. Arapoglou
IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications 2026
Open DOI ↗ Open arXiv ↗
Research overview

What question does this work ask?

Can a compact multi-port receive architecture improve multi-user satellite uplink performance while keeping RF complexity controlled?

What does the paper do?

This work introduces a compact ultra-massive receive architecture for satellite communications, activating multiple ports to combine desired signal components while controlling interference.

Figure 1 from the open arXiv version: conceptual multi-user satellite uplink system with compact multi-port receive arrays onboard the satellite.

Main ideas

  • Develops a compact array architecture tailored to multi-user satellite uplink reception.
  • Derives analytical characterizations of signal power, interference power, outage probability, and ergodic rate.
  • Provides physical insights into compact-array operation and beamforming gain.
